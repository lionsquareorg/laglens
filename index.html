About LagLens
LagLens Version: 2.1.0 (Free)
Developer Contact: [developer-email@example.com]

Core Principle: LagLens provides honest, objective round-trip time measurements to cloud endpoints. It does not manipulate network routes, does not claim to bypass cellular power states, and does not speed up your connection.

How it measures: Probes use fetch() with mode: "no-cors", cache: "no-store", unique cache-busting tokens, and a 3000ms timeout. Initial warm-up samples are discarded to keep averages accurate, and all metrics come from the same ring buffer.

Your data stays on your phone: Zero accounts, zero ads, zero analytics trackers.
Privacy Policy
Your data stays on your phone:

1. No accounts, no ads, no analytics: We do not track you or record your usage.

2. Local storage only: All settings, threshold preferences, test history, outage logs, and comparison profiles are stored exclusively on your device in local storage.

3. Network requests: Test requests are sent only to the servers you select (Google, Cloudflare, or a custom URL). As with any web request, those destination servers can see your device's IP address in order to reply.

A standalone copy of this policy is also available at Privacy section.
